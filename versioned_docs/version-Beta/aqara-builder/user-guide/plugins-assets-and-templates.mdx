# Plugins, Assets and Templates

Goal: Check resources, assign plugins to Studio and understand account entitlements and preferences.


## Browse and Obtain Plugins

1. Open **Plugins**, browse categories or search.
2. Inspect supported platform, version, instructions and release notes.
3. At **Get Plugin** or the acquisition entry, check eligibility, quantity and requirements, then follow the flow.
4. Check the acquisition result in assets.

<img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-builder/zh/22-plugin-detail.png" alt="Plugin details and instructions" style={{ width: '70%' }} />

*Browsing and the acquisition entry were checked; acquisition was not submitted.*

## My Assets and Assignment

1. Open **My Assets** in the current environment.
2. Inspect **Available to assign**, **Assigned** and **Expired**.
3. Select **Assign**, choose the project and Studio, and check quantity and destination.
4. After submitting in an applicable workflow, verify quantities, assignment records and use on Studio.

<img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-builder/zh/24-assets.png" alt="My Assets and assignable quantities" style={{ width: '70%' }} />

*The assignment dialog was opened but not submitted. Quantity changes and target results remain unverified.*

## Discover and Reuse Templates

Preview resources in available template/market entries and check their type before reuse. For space templates, inspect rooms/devices; for interfaces, inspect pages/bindings. Save in the correct project before further editing.
