# Matter

:::tip
This document is only applicable to the **hub-integrated model** of Aqara Studio.
:::

This document explains how to connect Matter devices to Aqara Studio.

:::tip
Aqara Studio supports the integration of Matter devices from Aqara and third-party brands.
:::

## Process

1. In the left sidebar, click to enter the **Devices** page. Select `Matter` to open the management page, and click the **Add Device** button.   
    ![Select Matter and click the Add Device button](https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Matter-AddDevice.png)
2. Click the **Configuration** button. In the pop-up window, enter the Wi-Fi information required for the sub-device to connect, and save the settings.   
    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Matter-ConfigureWiFi.png" alt="Configure Wi-Fi information" />
3. Select the gateway to which you want to bind the sub-device, and enter the device's 11-digit Matter pairing code.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Matter-SelectGatewayAndInputPairingCode.png" alt="Select a gateway and enter the pairing code" />

4. Put the sub-device into pairing mode, and click **Verify and Add**.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Matter-VerifyAndAdd.png" alt="Verify and Add" />

5. After the sub-device is successfully connected, it will appear in the **Device added** list.

## FAQ

### How do I connect Aqara devices that support dual-protocol or multi-protocol by Matter?

If your Aqara device supports Matter as well as other protocols, please follow these steps to connect via Matter:

- If the device is already in Matter mode, you can connect following the process described in this document.
- If the device uses another protocol, first switch the device to Matter mode on the Aqara Home App or Aqara Life App. After switching, follow this document's process to connect.
