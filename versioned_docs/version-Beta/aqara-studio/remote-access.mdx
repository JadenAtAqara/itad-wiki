# Remote Access

## Feature Overview

By default, you can only access Aqara Studio within your local area network (LAN). When you need to connect from an external network, you can use **remote access** to securely manage your smart home devices and control systems from anywhere.

## Prerequisites

Before using remote access, please ensure that you have [connected to Aqara Cloud](./cloud.mdx).

## Steps to Access

1. Visit the [Aqara Builder](https://builder.aqara.com) website, and navigate to **Builder Lab** from the left sidebar.

    ![Enter Builder Lab](https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/PluginCenter-EnterBuilderLab.png)

2. From the left sidebar, go to **Spaces**, then select the **Region** you chose when logging in with your Aqara account during [Cloud Connect](./cloud.mdx).

    Switch to the corresponding **Space**, find the device that matches your Aqara Studio IP, and click its **Connect** button to enter the remote access interface of that Aqara Studio.

    ![Connect to Aqara Studio](https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/RemoteAccess-Connect.png)

    :::tip
    You can also use an existing local user account to log in to the remote access address. The permissions granted will be exactly the same as those for local access. For details, please refer to [Local User Management - Local User Roles](./user.mdx#local-user-roles).
    :::

## Invite Other Users

1. To invite other Aqara users to access your Aqara Studio, first go to [Aqara Builder Lab](https://builder.aqara.com/lab) and create a new space (not **My Home**).

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Builder-NewSpace.png" alt="Create a space" />

    Click the space dropdown to see the newly created space.

2. Next, migrate your Aqara Studio from the **My Home** space to the newly created space.

    Find your Aqara Studio, and click the **Transfer Ownership** button.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Builder-TransferOwnership.png" alt="Transfer Aqara Studio" />

    In the popup, select the newly created space and click **Next**.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Builder-SelectSpace.png" alt="Select a space" style={{width: "60%"}} />

    Confirm the transfer details in the popup, then click **Confirm and Transfer** to complete the migration.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Builder-ConfirmTransfer.png" alt="Confirm transfer" style={{width: "60%"}}/>

3. From the space dropdown, select **Manage Spaces**.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Builder-ManageSpace.png" alt="Manage spaces" />

4. In the **Space** popup, select the newly created space and click **Invite Members**.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Builder-SpaceList.png" alt="Select the newly created space in the space list and invite members" />

5. In the popup, enter the **email address** of an Aqara user, and assign their **access** (`Operator` or `Admin`; for permission details, see [Remote User Roles](#remote-user-roles)).

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Builder-FillInAccountAndAccess.png" alt="Enter an email address and assign permission in the popup" style={{width: "50%"}} />

6. After the **invited user** receives the invitation email, they click the link in the email to go to [Aqara Builder](https://builder.aqara.com).

    Enter **Builder Lab** from the left menu. If there is a new invitation, an in-app notification will appear; click **Agree** to join the space.

    ![In-app notification for a new invitation](https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Builder-InvitationNotification.png)

7. After the **invited user** goes to **Space** from the left sidebar, they find the space they just joined, locate the device that matches your Aqara Studio IP, and click its **Connect** button to access your Aqara Studio remotely.

    ![Invited user accesses Aqara Studio](https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Builder-RemoteAccessForOtherUser.png)
