# Screens

This document explains in detail how to configure and publish Aqara screen devices with [Panels](#configure-and-publish-a-panel) and [Standby](#configure-and-publish-a-standby), helping you flexibly customize display effects and operating modes.

## Configure and Publish A Panel

1. On the **Devices** page, find **Aqara Hub** in the **Protocols and Devices** tree on the left, select your target screen device, switch to the **Panel configuration** tab, and click **Add Panel**.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Screens-CreateAPanel.png" alt="Add a panel" />

2. In the **New Panel** pop-up window, select a template, name the panel, and click the **Create** button.

3. You will now enter the panel editing page, where you can configure the panel’s components, templates, layers, styles, and properties.

    :::tip
    If components need to be linked to automation or device data, please ensure all related data sources are configured; otherwise, publishing may fail.
    :::

    After finishing, click the **Save** button.

4. Once editing is complete, exit the panel editing page and return to the **Panel configuration** tab of the target screen device. 

    Locate the newly added panel in the list and click **... > Preview** to view the panel effect.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Screens-PreviewAPanel.png" alt="Preview the panel" />

5. After confirming the effect, click the **Publish** button.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Screens-ClickPublish.png" alt="Click Publish" />

6. In the **Publish** pop-up window, select the panels to be displayed, adjust the order, and click the **Confirm publish** button.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Screens-SelectPanelsAndAdjustTheOrderAndComfirmPublish.png" alt="Select panels, adjust the order, and confirm publish" />

    After successful publication, you can view the panel on the screen device.
    
## Configure and Publish A Standby

1. On the **Devices** page, find **Aqara Hub** in the **Protocols and Devices** tree on the left, select your target screen device, switch to the **Standby configuration** tab, and click **Add Standby**.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Screens-CreateAStandby.png" alt="Add a standby" />

2. In the **New Standby** pop-up window, select a template, name the standby, and click the **Create** button.

3. You will now enter the standby editing page, where you can configure the standby’s components, templates, layers, styles, and properties.

4. After editing, exit the standby editing page and return to the **Standby configuration** tab of the target screen device.

    Locate the newly added standby in the list and click **... > Preview** to view the standby effect.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Screens-PreviewStandby.png" alt="Preview Standby" />

5. After confirming the effect, click the **Publish** button.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Screens-StandbyClickPublish.png" alt="Click Publish Standby" />

6. In the **Publish** pop-up window, select a standby and click the **Confirm publish** button.

    <img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/en/Screens-SelectAStandbyAndConfirmPublish.png" alt="Select Standby and Confirm Publish" style={{width: '70%'}} />

    After successful publication, you will be able to view the standby effect on the screen device.
