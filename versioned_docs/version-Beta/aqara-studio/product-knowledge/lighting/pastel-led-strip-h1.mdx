### Installation Precautions

#### LED Strip Wiring

- Please confirm power is turned off before installation to avoid electrical accidents; perform wiring and installation operations in powered-off state;
- Input voltage must not exceed: 24V (+/-10%).
- Separate routing of lighting circuits and other power circuits (such as air conditioning or high-power motor equipment).

#### LED Strip Installation

- Light source of this product should be replaced by manufacturer or its service agent or personnel with similar qualifications;
- This product only supports use with specified controller; usage method: LED Strip + [Aqara Pastel Light Strip Controller H1](./pastel-light-strip-controller-h1.mdx) + DC24V power supply;
- Before maintaining or installing LED strip, confirm power has been disconnected, confirm wiring is correct before turning on power. (Risk of electric shock)
- Under no circumstances should LED strip be covered by thermal insulation pads or similar materials; ensure at least 100mm space around product.
- During operation and after shutdown, LED strip is in high-temperature state; do not touch LED strip. (Risk of burns)
- Please do not disassemble or arbitrarily cut LED strip. (Risk of electric shock, fire, falling, malfunction)
- Please do not operate installation with wet hands. (Risk of electric shock)
- This LED strip power cord is Z-type connection; if flexible cord is damaged, this LED strip shall be scrapped.
- Cutting LED strip must be done at cutting mark positions on strip; minimum cutting length is 62.5mm;

## Maintenance

- Regularly wipe LED strip surface with dry soft cloth to avoid dust accumulation affecting light output;
- Do not use chemical solvents to clean LED strip;
- When not in use for extended periods, store in dry environment after disconnecting power.

## Troubleshooting Guide

### Issue 1: LED Strip Not Lighting

| Possible Cause | Solution |
| -- | -- |
| <ul><li>Incorrect power wiring, polarity reversed</li><li>Driver power supply DC24V no output or insufficient power</li><li>Abnormal connection between LED strip and controller</li><li>LED strip physical damage</li></ul> | <ol><li>Check if power wiring is correct, confirm polarity is correct;</li><li>Check if driver power supply DC24V is outputting normally and if power meets total power requirement of connected LED strip;</li><li>Check if connection between LED strip and controller output terminals (LED+/R/G/B/W) is normal;</li><li>Check if LED strip has physical damage; if damaged, replace damaged section according to cutting marks.</li></ol> |

### Issue 2: LED Strip Partially Not Lighting / Local Dark Areas

| Possible Cause | Solution |
| -- | -- |
| <ul><li>Incorrect cutting position</li><li>Insulating tape not added at LED strip end causing short circuit</li><li>Excessive bending of LED strip causing internal circuit damage</li></ul> | <ol><li>Confirm cutting position is at LED strip cutting mark line (minimum cutting length 62.5mm);</li><li>Add insulating tape at LED strip end to isolate conductive contact, avoid positive/negative short circuit;</li><li>Check if LED strip is excessively bent or twisted (minimum bending radius 50mm).</li></ol> |

### Issue 3: Abnormal Dimming / Flickering

| Possible Cause | Solution |
| -- | -- |
| <ul><li>Driver power supply power mismatch</li><li>Abnormal signal connection between LED strip and controller</li><li>Signal interference</li></ul> | <ol><li>Check if driver power supply power meets total power requirement of connected LED strip (>= LED strip total power);</li><li>Confirm LED strip and controller wiring is correct and contact is good;</li><li>Check if signal interference exists.</li></ol> |

## Special Notes

### Coordinated Usage Instructions

- [Aqara Pastel Light Strip Controller H1](./pastel-light-strip-controller-h1.mdx) and Aqara Pastel LED Strip H1 are sold and used as coordinated set; controller is platform solution;
- Controller input voltage is DC 24V, cannot be directly connected to mains, must be used with power supply outputting DC 24V; driver power supply output power must be greater than or equal to connected LED strip power;
- Controller has only one output, does not support splitting into multiple channels for independent control (different from dual color temperature LED strip module's two-channel independent control).

## FAQ

### How to cut LED strip? What is the maximum length that can be connected?

Cutting LED strip must be done at cutting mark positions on strip; minimum cutting length is 62.5mm. LED strip is 10 meters per roll, 10W/meter; actual connectable length depends on controller output power (480W Max) and power supply power; driver power supply output power must be greater than or equal to total power of connected LED strip.

### What precautions should be taken during LED strip installation?

Cut off power before installation; clean mounting surface and fully press when using 3M adhesive for fixing; coordinate with mounting clips, aluminum profiles for auxiliary fixing when necessary; add insulating tape at LED strip end to avoid short circuit; minimum radius 50mm for bending operation, do not twist LED strip.
