## Product Overview

The **Aqara Track Light V1** is a full-spectrum dual color temperature smart luminaire based on the Zigbee communication protocol. It includes track spotlights, track grille lights, and track floodlights, suitable for recessed tracks, ultra-thin surface-mounted tracks, and various installation methods. When paired with an **Aqara Gateway**, it supports dimming and color temperature control for individual or grouped luminaires, enabling scene-based control and automation with other smart devices.

Luminaires use a **48V safe low-voltage power supply**, where the track power supply converts 220-240V household AC to 48V DC constant voltage output. The **Track Spotlight V1** model features **CRI Ra98**, supporting **2700K-6000K dual color temperature adjustment**. Grille lights and floodlights are available in 30cm/60cm or 12-head/24-head specifications, with power options of 12W and 24W, meeting different scenario requirements for accent lighting, wall washing, and ambient lighting.

- This device must be used with an **Aqara Gateway** for full functionality support. Certain specific features are only supported within the **Aqara ecosystem**.
- The track power supply, luminaires, IoT adapter, etc., must be installed according to the instruction manual. Track and luminaire power contacts require the use of **Aqara dedicated rail system**.

### Installation Precautions

:::caution
Installation involves high-voltage electrical work and must be performed by qualified professionals in accordance with electrical codes and the product instruction manual.
:::
- This product is for indoor use only; do not use in humid environments or outdoors;
- Keep away from moisture; do not splash water or other liquids onto this product;
- Do not place this product near heat sources;
- Do not attempt to repair this product yourself; this work should be performed by authorized professionals;
- The light source in this luminaire is non-replaceable; when the light source reaches end-of-life, replace the entire luminaire;
- This luminaire uses a non-standard rail connector for power connection and must be used with dedicated rails. Do not use rail systems from other manufacturers, as this may cause safety hazards;
- This luminaire is a Class III fixture; its control gear must be SELV (Safety Extra-Low Voltage);
- Disconnect power before installation;
- Insert the luminaire power contact portion into the installed track slot, ensuring reliable contact.

:::caution
Under no circumstances should the luminaire be covered by thermal insulation materials or similar materials.
:::

### General Installation Procedure

1.  **Track Installation**: According to the selected installation method (recessed track / ultra-thin surface-mounted track), fix the **Aqara dedicated rail** to the ceiling or wall surface;
    
2.  **Power Connection**: Connect the track power supply to 220-240V~ household AC, and connect the output terminal to the track;
    
3.  **Luminaire Installation**: After confirming power disconnection, insert the luminaire power contact portion into the installed track slot;
    
4.  **Power-On Test**: Switch on power and confirm that the luminaire operates normally.

The device defaults to **Zigbee protocol** for network pairing. When the device enters pairing mode, it can be added to **Aqara Studio** or **Aqara Life App** for use.

## FAQ

### What types of luminaires are included in the Track Light V1 Series?

The Track Light V1 Series includes three types: **Track Spotlights**, **Track Grille Lights**, and **Track Floodlights**, along with 100W / 200W **Track Power Supplies** and **IoT Adapters**.

### What installation methods does Track Light V1 support?

Supports two installation methods: **Recessed Track** and **Ultra-thin Surface-mounted Track**. After the track power supply converts 220-240V AC to 48V DC constant voltage output, luminaires draw power by inserting their power contacts into the track slot.

### Does Track Light V1 require Aqara dedicated rails?

Yes. This luminaire uses non-standard rail connectors for power connection and must be used with dedicated rails. Do not use rail systems from other manufacturers, as this may cause safety risks.

### How to enter pairing mode?

When the device is in unpaired state, it will automatically enter pairing mode upon powering on.

### How to reset the device?

Cycle power on/off 5 times consecutively, ending in ON state. Wait for the light to breathe twice and flash once — the device has completed reset and entered pairing status.

### How to verify effective communication distance between device and gateway?

Use the power switch to cycle power on/off 3 times consecutively. If the gateway emits a prompt tone, it indicates effective communication between the device and gateway.
