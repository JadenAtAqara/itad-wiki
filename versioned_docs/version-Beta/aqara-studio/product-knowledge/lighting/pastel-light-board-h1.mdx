## Product Overview

Aqara Pastel Light Board H1 is a large-size quad-color (RGBW) LED blockchain light board specifically designed for light film/light box scenarios. It adopts 246 x 496mm rectangular aluminum substrate PCB design (with screw holes), featuring high-efficiency assembly and superior heat dissipation. Product built-in 128 units of 2835 LEDs, single board with 8-pixel independent control, supports red, green, blue, white four-color independent PWM dimming, capable of achieving full gamut color mixing and precise color temperature adjustment.

Suitable for stretch ceiling, advertising light box, home decoration, engineering projects, buildings, hotels, shopping malls, decorative lighting and other light film/light box lighting scenarios.

- This device requires use with Aqara gateway and [Lighting Scene Controller AW100L](./lighting-scene-controller-aw100l.mdx) for full functionality support. Certain specific features are only supported within the Aqara ecosystem.

### Installation Precautions

- Please confirm power is turned off before installation to avoid electrical accidents.
- Perform wiring and installation operations in a powered-off state.
- The recommended maximum blockchain cascade quantity is 4 boards; power reinjection is required if exceeded (when reinforcing power for 4 boards, the single board total power should not exceed 36W).
- Pay attention to signal arrow direction during wiring; do not reverse, otherwise the entire light will not work.
- When using maximum power, the blockchain light source requires sufficient heat dissipation (the light box must have an aluminum heat dissipation back panel).
- Do not bend or excessively pull light board connection cables.
    
### Installation Steps

Light boards need to be used together with scene controller. Complete installation steps and functions see Lighting Scene Controller AW100L.

## Maintenance

- Regularly wipe light board surface with a dry soft cloth to avoid dust accumulation affecting light output.
- Do not use chemical solvents to clean the light board.
- When not in use for extended periods, store in a dry environment after disconnecting power.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between small board and large board?

Small board (70 x 70mm) has 16 LEDs, RGBW four colors fully on 920lm/board, 8W/board, maximum cascade 12 boards, with screw holes for flexible installation; Large board (496 x 246mm) has 128 LEDs, single board 8-pixel independent control, RGBW four colors fully on 4800lm/board, 48W/board, maximum cascade 4 boards, higher installation efficiency. Both support RS-485 wired communication, 16-bit high-precision dimming, AI scene generation and Aqara whole-home ecosystem linkage.

### Can multiple large boards be used in series?

Yes. Large boards support cascading, maximum cascade quantity is 4 boards. Power reinjection required if exceeded (when reinforcing power for 4 boards, single board total power not exceeding 36W).

### What should I pay attention to when wiring large boards?

Pay attention to signal arrow direction during wiring; do not reverse, otherwise entire light will not work. Large boards come with 9pin cables (different from small board's 5pin), must use matching 9pin splicing cables and power reinjection cables.

### What are the heat dissipation requirements for large boards?

When using maximum power, blockchain light source requires sufficient heat dissipation, light box must have aluminum heat dissipation back panel. Large board single board power is relatively high (48W), with stricter heat dissipation requirements, recommend reserving adequate ventilation space.

### Can large boards and small boards be mixed?

Single RS-485 controller single channel can drive small boards or large boards separately, theoretically just don't mix on same channel, but recommend using same specification boards within same light box/light film project to ensure light source spacing, brightness and color consistency.
