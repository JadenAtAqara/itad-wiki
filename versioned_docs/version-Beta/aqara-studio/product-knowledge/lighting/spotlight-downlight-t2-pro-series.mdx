## Product Highlights

- **Full-spectrum LED chips** with **Ra96+ high CRI**, **SDCM < 5** color consistency, delivering excellent color uniformity.
- **2700K-6000K wide-range color temperature adjustment** with **0.01% million-level dimming depth** for smooth, refined dimming performance.

- Supports **Zigbee**, **Thread**, and **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)** protocols; can connect to **Aqara Gateway** for scene-based control and device automation.

## Product Overview

The **Spotlight T2 Pro Series** is Aqara's smart spotlight/downlight product line designed for residential ambient lighting. As an upgraded version of the best-selling **T2 series** (launched August 2025), it adopts the **MG24 main control module** and **Toyoda full-spectrum LED chips**, with upgraded software platform and latest interaction design. The series covers three installation forms: **75mm narrow-frame standard model**, **75mm frameless flush-mount model**, and **55mm framed standard model**, totaling **13 SKUs** (dual power options of 12W / 8W), along with accessories such as matte white reflectors and flush-mount trim rings.

Based on **Zigbee** and **Thread** communication protocols, the device can be installed recessed into ceilings for individual or grouped dimming and color temperature control. When paired with an **Aqara Gateway**, it enables scene-based control and automation with other smart devices. The light source features **Toyoda full-spectrum LED chips**, supporting **2700K-6000K wide-range color temperature** and **0.01% million-level dimming depth**, with **Ra96+ high CRI**, **SDCM < 5 color consistency**, and **UGR < 16 low glare** — significantly improved light quality compared to the previous T2 generation.

- This device must be used with an **Aqara Gateway** for full functionality support. Certain specific features are only supported within the **Aqara ecosystem**.

## Usage Instructions

### Device Pairing

The device defaults to **Zigbee protocol** for network pairing. When the device enters pairing mode, it can be added to **Aqara Studio** or **Aqara Life App** for use.

:::tip
When the device is not paired to a network, it will automatically enter pairing mode upon powering on (the light breathes twice). The pairing window lasts for 60 seconds. If the pairing time expires, re-power the device to re-enter pairing mode.
:::

#### Adding to Aqara Studio

Based on the current protocol used by the device, please refer to the corresponding documentation:

| Protocol | Documentation |
| --- | --- |
| Zigbee | [Zigbee Device Integration Guide](./../../device-integration/zigbee.mdx) |
| Matter | [Matter Device Integration Guide](./../../device-integration/matter.mdx) |

#### Adding to Aqara Life App

1. Download the **Aqara Life App** (scan the QR code on the instruction manual, or search and download from Apple App Store / Google Play).
2. Open the App, navigate to "Home", tap the "+" icon in the top-right corner to enter "Add Device" page, and follow the App prompts to complete adding.

### Firmware Update

You can update the device firmware to the latest version in **Aqara Studio** or **Aqara Life App** before use.

:::tip
If you integrate the device into a third-party ecosystem (such as Apple, Google, or Amazon) and cannot obtain the latest firmware, you can add the device in **Aqara Life App** and perform two "protocol switch" operations (e.g., switch Thread → Zigbee, then switch back to Thread) to pull the latest cloud firmware for upgrade.
:::

### Device Reset / Factory Restore

| Operation | Method | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device Reset | Long press reset button on driver for 5+ seconds, OR cycle power on/off 5 times consecutively (last cycle ends with power ON) | Light breathes twice; reset successful. Device clears network pairing only and re-enters pairing status. |
| Factory Restore | Tap reset button on driver 10 times, OR cycle power on/off 10 times consecutively (last cycle ends with power ON) | Light breathes twice; factory restore successful. Device clears all configuration data and re-enters pairing status. |
| Protocol Switch | Remove device in **Aqara Life App** first, then long press reset button for 5 seconds to reset, enter pairing process, tap "Switch Protocol" on device pairing screen | Note: Protocol switching is equivalent to factory restore and will clear all device settings and data. |

## FAQ

### What specifications are available in the Spotlight T2 Pro Series?

The T2 Pro Series includes **13 SKUs** across three installation forms: **75mm narrow-frame standard models** (12W, spotlights at 15°/24°/36° + downlights at 60°, black/white optional), **75mm frameless flush-mount models** (12W, spotlights at 24°/36°/55°), and **55mm framed standard models** (8W, spotlights at 24°/36°/55°, black only). Accessories include matte white reflectors and flush-mount trim rings.

### What are the differences between T2 Pro and the previous T2? Can they be mixed?

T2 Pro features upgrades across multiple aspects: module (**MG21 → MG24**), LED chips (**Toyoda Ra90 → Toyoda Full-Spectrum Ra96+**), power (**10W → 12W**), reset method (added physical button), and now supports **Bluetooth Low Energy**. The previous T2 model will be discontinued upon T2 Pro launch. Due to differences in power and CRI, there are variations in light color and brightness between T2 Pro and T2; mixing them in the same space is **not recommended**.

### How to enter pairing mode?

When the device is in unpaired state, it will automatically enter pairing mode upon powering on — no need to cycle power for reset. If adding fails, move the gateway closer to this product, power on, and try again.

### How to verify effective communication distance between device and gateway?

Use the power switch to cycle the driver power on/off 3 times consecutively. If the gateway emits a prompt tone, it indicates effective communication between the device and gateway.

### How to reset the device?

Two methods are supported: cycle power on/off 5 times consecutively (last cycle ends with power ON), or **long press the reset button on the driver**. After successful reset, the device clears network pairing only and re-enters pairing status. (Refer to instruction manual for specific button operations.)

### Does the device support power failure memory function?

Yes. The device defaults to "restore state at power loss". Users can configure the power-on behavior in the App settings to: light ON after power restore, light OFF after power restore, or **toggle** (opposite to state prior to power loss).
