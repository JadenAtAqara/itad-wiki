# Overview

Aqara Space App is a mobile application built for **professionals (installers)** within the **[Aqara Studio](./../aqara-studio/overview/introduction.mdx)** product ecosystem, positioned as a field deployment and implementation delivery tool for smart spaces. Its core purpose is to take spatial solutions completed online on the platform and bring them to life efficiently as real, usable smart spaces: installers can use it to complete Studio connection, device onboarding, solution deployment, AI-powered on-site fine-tuning, and delivery acceptance.

In contrast to the **[Aqara Life App](./../aqara-life/overview.mdx)**, which serves end users, **Aqara Space** serves the first half of the smart space delivery journey—it is not an entry point for daily use, but a tool for turning solutions into reality. Once delivery is complete, homeowners use the Aqara Life App for daily use, and the two apps together form a complete journey from delivery to use.

## Aqara Space's Position in the Studio Ecosystem

Within the Studio ecosystem, spatial solutions are created online by designers and creators on the **[Aqara Builder](./../aqara-builder/overview.mdx)** platform, while projects and work orders are managed by business systems; Aqara Space is responsible for on-site surveying, installation, deployment, and delivery implementation.

Advanced spatial configuration is mainly completed on the Aqara Builder or Studio platform, while Aqara Space focuses on bringing solutions to life on site: connecting the on-site environment and devices, precisely deploying solutions to Studio, fine-tuning deployment results, and driving projects through acceptance and delivery.

## Core Capabilities of Aqara Space

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| Access | After signing in, installers can access the work orders and projects assigned to them, and review the solution overview and layout design at any time—fully prepared before arriving on site. |
| Connect | Automatically discover and connect the Studio hub on the same local network, with static IP configuration support to adapt to various on-site network environments. <br />Smart devices via Thread, Matter, BLE, and more can be onboarded with ease and connected into Studio. |
| Deploy | Precisely map solution devices to real devices, with a device-finder feature to quickly locate physical devices; <br />Once every device is matched, deploy the entire solution to Studio with one tap—turning the online-designed solution into a real, usable smart space. |
| Fine-tune | Use the AI Agent to adjust scenes and automations in natural language and troubleshoot intelligently, fine-tuning deployment results on site to ensure delivery quality. |
| Deliver | Complete implementation checks and delivery acceptance with the self-check and acceptance features. |

## Usage Model for Professionals

Typical usage scenarios of the Aqara Space App:

- After receiving a project, service provider installers use Aqara Space to access their assigned work orders and project information, connect Studio on site, complete device onboarding and solution deployment, and finish implementation checks and delivery acceptance with the self-check and acceptance features.
